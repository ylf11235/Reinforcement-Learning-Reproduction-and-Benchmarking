# Third-party source

This backend is based on `nikhilbarhate99/PPO-PyTorch`
(https://github.com/nikhilbarhate99/PPO-PyTorch), obtained as a snapshot of
the upstream `master` archive on 2026-09-08. The upstream repository was
at commit `728cce83d7ab628fe2634eabcdf3239997eb81dd` (2023-12-08).

The upstream MIT license is included in `LICENSE`. The Atari CNN and runner
are an independent adaptation; the PPO update remains the upstream discrete
CartPole-style Monte-Carlo implementation.
